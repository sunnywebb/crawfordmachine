Why our slips

Tested against the competition. Nobody came close.

A slip has one job: hold the load in the well without slipping, cracking, or deforming. We heat-treat every set, then pressure test them. When we put ours up against competitors' slips under the same test, ours held after the others gave out.

  1. Machined in Titusville on our own lathes and CNC equipment, not outsourced.
  2. Heat-treated for a hard gripping surface over a tough core.
  3. Pressure tested before they leave the shop.

About the shop

Father and son, same shop floor

Crawford Machine has been machining in Titusville since 1950. Today it is owned by Bill Webb and run day to day with his son BJ Webb. When you call, you talk to the person who will run your part. No sales desk, no hand-offs.

Titusville sits a mile from the Drake Well, and this town has been making oilfield machinery since 1860. We are part of that lineage: a small shop that serves the producers, service companies, and manufacturers of northwest Pennsylvania, and ships anywhere a part needs to go.
